# Engineering Effectiveness Assessment

A 2-week engagement that produces a clear view of how your engineering organisation is actually performing, and where to focus first.

## What we assess

Six dimensions, each scored with a confidence level based on what measurement actually exists in the organisation:

1. **Legibility.** Can the org see itself? Service ownership, catalogue completeness, team membership and contact points, and where knowledge and understanding are concentrated across the organisation.
2. **Flow health.** Deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, MTTR.
3. **Stack coherence.** How fragmented is the technology estate? Incompatible stacks and platforms increase cognitive load, make it harder to move engineers between areas, and create inconsistency in how standards are applied.
4. **Absorption capacity.** Is the org set up to improve over time? Covers the pace of delivery, how much cognitive load engineers are carrying, and whether there are real mechanisms for strategy to reach teams and ground-level insight to reach leadership.
5. **Toolchain integration.** Are the key systems connected and feeding into measurement mechanics, or are signals locked inside isolated tools?
6. **Quality practice.** Whether there is a testing strategy and quality is built into delivery. Covers test coverage across the stack, the balance of automated and manual testing, and whether QA capability is embedded in teams or held in a separate function with its own cost and dependency profile.

## How it works

The engagement runs over two weeks. The first week is discovery and structured conversations; 

* mapping where metrics live, what coverage they have

* talking to engineering managers, tech leads, and a sample of engineers. 

The second week is analysis, scoring, and report.

The output is calibrated to what measurement actually exists in the organisation. Each dimension is scored with a confidence level that reflects the quality of the underlying data.

## What you get

1. Engineering effectiveness scorecard, organisation-wide and per team
2. Measurement source map; what we assessed, where the metrics came from, and the confidence level for each signal
3. Measurement maturity rating on a 0–5 scale
4. Prioritised recommendations
5. 90-day plan skeleton
