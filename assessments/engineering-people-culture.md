# Engineering People & Culture Assessment

A 2-week engagement that assesses whether the people model in your engineering organisation is set up for the work it needs to do — from how expectations and career progression are defined, to whether the culture gives people the conditions to perform at their best.

## What we assess

Five dimensions:

1. **Role clarity and expectations.** Do people know what is expected of them, and have those expectations kept pace with how the organisation has changed?
2. **Career framework alignment.** Does the framework support the actual behaviours and skills the work requires? Covers whether it connects through to recruitment, reward, and development in practice.
3. **Strategic communication.** Do engineers hold the strategy in their minds? Do they understand why the work they're doing matters, and does that understanding stay current as strategy evolves?
4. **Voice and inclusion.** Do engineers feel genuinely listened to, and does their input reach the people who make decisions?
5. **Enabling environment.** What the culture at leadership level looks like in practice, and whether it creates the conditions for the engineering organisation to do its best work.

## How it works

The engagement runs over two weeks. The first week is document review and structured conversations;

* reviewing role definitions, career frameworks, and any existing people or culture data
* talking to engineering leaders, people managers, and a cross-section of engineers across the organisation

The second week is analysis, synthesis, and report. Findings draw from both what frameworks and policies describe and what people actually experience day to day.

## What you get

1. People model assessment: where expectations, career frameworks, and culture are aligned and where the gaps are
2. Communication and inclusion review: how well strategy reaches teams and how well ground-level insight reaches leadership
3. Leadership environment summary: what the culture at leadership level is creating for the rest of the organisation
4. Prioritised recommendations
5. 90-day plan skeleton
