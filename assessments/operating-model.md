# Operating Model & Org Design Assessment

A 2-week engagement that establishes whether your organisation is structured to achieve what it is trying to do, and where the design needs to develop to support your next stage.

## What we assess

Six dimensions:

1. **Operating model clarity.** Is there a documented model, does it match how the organisation actually works, and do people at different levels describe it the same way?
2. **Structural fit.** Is the design set up to support where the organisation is going?
3. **Team topology.** How teams are set up, how they interact, and where handoffs are creating friction.
4. **Decision rights.** Who decides what, at what level, and where decisions are getting stuck.
5. **Workforce composition.** Are the right capabilities in the right places? Skills profile, role balance, and whether the workforce model has a clear purpose behind it.
6. **Tech strategy alignment.** Does a tech strategy exist, and does it map to what the operating model needs technology to do?

## How it works

The engagement runs over two weeks. The first week is document review and structured conversations;

* working through org charts, operating model papers, strategy documents, and any existing tech strategy
* talking to leadership sponsors, people managers, tech leads, and a cross-section of the organisation

The second week is analysis, synthesis, and report. 

Each dimension is assessed across both what the documentation says and what people describe in practice, with findings mapped to where the two diverge.

## What you get

1. Operating model assessment: how the current design holds up against where the organisation is going
2. Structural recommendations
3. Tech strategy gap analysis: whether technology is positioned to enable the operating model
4. Prioritised recommendations
5. 90-day plan skeleton
