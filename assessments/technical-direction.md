# Technical Direction Assessment

A 2-week engagement that establishes whether the technology estate has a coherent direction, and whether that direction is achievable from where things actually are today.

## What we assess

Five dimensions:

1. **Estate visibility.** Does the organisation have a clear, current picture of its technology landscape? What exists, what state it is in, and where significant technical debt sits.
2. **Architecture coherence.** Are architectural decisions being made deliberately and recorded, or is the architecture drifting through accumulated choices nobody revisited?
3. **Platform and tooling strategy.** Is there a deliberate platform layer? Are build vs buy decisions made with a consistent framework, or case by case without one?
4. **Direction credibility.** Is there a target state, is it clearly articulated, and is there a believable path from where things are now to where they need to be?
5. **AI position.** Does the organisation have a considered position on AI in the technical stack, or is adoption reactive and uncoordinated?

## How it works

The engagement runs over two weeks. The first week is document review and structured technical conversations;

* working through architecture documentation, ADRs, roadmaps, and any existing tech strategy
* talking to technical leads, architects, and engineering managers across the organisation

The second week is analysis, synthesis, and report.

Each dimension is assessed against both what documentation describes and what people are actually building toward, with findings that surface where those two things diverge.

## What you get

1. Technology direction assessment: current state, target state, and the credibility of the path between them
2. Architecture coherence review: where decisions are deliberate and where the architecture has drifted
3. AI position summary: the organisation's current stance and what a deliberate position would require
4. Prioritised recommendations
5. 90-day plan skeleton
